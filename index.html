About The Children’s Museum Jordan

The Children’s Museum Jordan is a non-profit educational institution launched by Her Majesty Queen Rania Al Abdullah in 2007. Its mission is to make learning enjoyable through play. The museum has more than 185 interactive indoor and outdoor exhibits and educational spaces, including an Art Studio, Tinker Studio, Library and Secret Garden.
